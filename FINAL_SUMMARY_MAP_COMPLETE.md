## 🗺️ WHAT THE FEATURE DOES

### Map Component
- Shows OpenStreetMap tiles
- Displays GPS marker at claim location
- Shows accuracy circle (GPS precision range)
- Displays coordinates and address

### Controls
- [+] Button: Zoom in
- [-] Button: Zoom out
- [⊙] Button: Center on marker
- Drag: Pan around map
- Scroll: Zoom in/out

- Mobile: 250px height
- Desktop: 200px height
- Touch-friendly on phones
- Mouse-friendly on desktop
