## 1. Introduction

Welcome to PODSafe ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our proof of delivery management application and services (collectively, the "Service").

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Personal Information You Provide

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:
- Register for an account
- Use the Service
- Contact us for support

**Information collected includes:**
- **Account Information:** Full name, email address, phone number, company name
- **Authentication Information:** Password (encrypted), authentication tokens
- **Profile Information:** User role (admin/driver), profile picture (optional)
- **Business Information:** Company registration details, business address

### 2.2 Information Automatically Collected

When you use the Service, we automatically collect certain information:

**Device Information:**
- Device type and model
- Operating system and version
- Unique device identifiers
- Mobile network information

**Usage Information:**
- App features used
- Time and date of access
- App crashes and errors (via Firebase Crashlytics)
- Performance metrics (via Firebase Analytics)

**Location Information:**
- GPS coordinates during delivery activities
- Delivery addresses
- Route information

**Note:** Location data is only collected when actively using delivery features and with your explicit permission.

### 2.3 Delivery & POD Information

**Customer Information:**
- Customer names and account numbers
- Delivery addresses
- Contact information

**Proof of Delivery Data:**
- Photographs of delivered goods
- Digital signatures
- Recipient names
- Delivery notes and timestamps
- GPS coordinates at delivery location

**Claims Information:**
- Claim descriptions and evidence (photos, documents)
- Driver statements and comments
- Resolution details

## 5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

### 5.1 Security Measures
- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit (SSL/TLS) and at rest
- **Authentication:** Secure password requirements and authentication tokens
- **Access Control:** Role-based access with company isolation
- **Monitoring:** Automated security monitoring and alerts
- **Backups:** Regular automated backups with encryption

### 5.2 Firebase Security
- Firestore security rules enforce multi-tenant isolation
- Storage rules validate file types and sizes
- Authentication required for all data access
- Server-side validation of all requests

**Note:** No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

---

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Active Account Data:** Retained while your account is active
- **Delivery Records:** Retained for 7 years for legal/audit purposes
- **POD Images:** Retained for 7 years for dispute resolution
- **Claims Data:** Retained for 7 years for legal compliance
- **Analytics Data:** Aggregated data retained indefinitely
- **Crash Reports:** Retained for 90 days

### 6.2 Deletion
When you request account deletion:
- Personal identifiable information is deleted within 30 days
- Delivery and POD records are anonymized but retained for legal compliance
- Backups are purged within 90 days

## 7. Your Privacy Rights

### 7.1 Rights Under POPIA (South Africa)
Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal information
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)
- **Objection:** Object to processing of your information
- **Restriction:** Request restriction of processing
- **Portability:** Request transfer of your data to another service
- **Withdraw Consent:** Withdraw consent at any time

### 7.2 Rights Under GDPR (If Applicable)
If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights under GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

### 7.3 Exercising Your Rights
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at:
- **Email:** privacy@podsafe.app (Update with your actual email)
