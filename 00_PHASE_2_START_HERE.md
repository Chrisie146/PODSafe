## ✨ What Was Built

### Create Claim Form
A new form allowing users to file insurance claims **without requiring evidence upfront**:
- Delivery search with autocomplete
- 15 claim types to choose from
- Optional photo upload (up to 5)
- Optional digital signature capture
- Full form validation
- Direct Firebase integration

### Upload Evidence Form
A new form allowing users to submit evidence **after a claim is filed**:
- Real-time dropdown of pending claims
- Claim information display
- Photo upload (up to 5)
- Digital signature capture
- Evidence status tracking
- Direct Firebase integration

### Tab System
A 3-tab interface within Claims Management:
- **Tab 1: All Claims** - Existing full functionality preserved
- **Tab 2: Create Claim** - New form for creating claims
- **Tab 3: Upload Evidence** - New form for submitting evidence
